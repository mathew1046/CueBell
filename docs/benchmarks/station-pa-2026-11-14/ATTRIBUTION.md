# Actual railway PA recordings

These are complete published field recordings of station loudspeakers and their
surroundings. Selection was completed before denoising results were available.
The recordists' descriptions identify the places, equipment, and capture dates
where available.

## Processing and provenance

- Downloaded Freesound's publicly available high-quality MP3 previews, which are
  lossy encodings of the recordists' uploads. The downloaded bytes and source
  pages are retained with SHA256 hashes in the recording manifest.
- Original HTML snapshots are retained locally and excluded from Git because
  the pages embed a third-party service token. The manifest preserves their
  original hashes; source URLs, attribution and downloaded audio remain available
  in the repository.
- Converted each complete preview to mono, 16 kHz PCM16. No noise synthesis,
  artificial mixing, gain adjustment, speech generation, or excerpt selection
  was performed by this benchmark.
- The "after" files are produced by CueBell's native GTCRN processor on the
  connected Android phone. Their original length and causal 16 ms alignment
  delay are retained.
- Some pages categorize automated PA speech as "Processed / Synthetic"; the
  recordists explicitly describe microphones capturing the announcements at
  stations. The material being tested is that field capture, including its
  environmental sound.
- Uploader-provided platform/time/destination facts are partial annotations.
  There is no independently verified complete transcript or paired clean audio.
- The recordists and Freesound do not endorse CueBell or these observations.
